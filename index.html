HOW TO PLAY
  • Aim: drag back from your glowing active magnet to set angle and power, then release to launch.
  • Knockouts: magnets stop right at the table's edge on their own — only a hit from another magnet can send one flying off.
  • Magnetism: nearby magnets gently pull toward each other, then repel hard on contact.
  • ★ Power-up: land on the corner coins to power up and hit harder until your next flip!
  • Teams: in Teams mode, active magnets are randomly split into two sides — reshuffled every match.
  • Hazards: toggle Obstacles or the Gravitational Well on the home screen for extra chaos — neither ever blocks the corner coins.
